About Me

I’m a 23-year-old game designer with a passion for understanding player psychology and crafting meaningful player experiences. I’m fascinated by how mechanics, systems, and feedback loops influence player behavior, emotion, and interpretation.


As a writer at heart, I approach design through the lens of narrative and theme—analyzing how gameplay systems reinforce dramatic intent rather than exist independently of it. I believe strong games emerge when mechanics, story, and presentation are in constant dialogue with one another.


This perspective has pushed me to explore UI and diegetic design, sound, art, and level design, allowing me to think holistically about how every design choice contributes to immersion, emotional resonance, and emergent play.
