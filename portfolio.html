About Me

I am a game designer who focuses on a systems-driven design approach, treating tools like player movement & logical strategy as affordances and instances like health and money as resources. Games are all about the harmonization of these affordances using the resources made available by the system. When these different systems intertwine and flow together well , they give rise to Emergent Gameplay, which serves as the foundation for meaningful and engaging experiences.
