Design Goal
The goal of this prototype was to capture the nostalgic immediacy of classic arcade games like Pong while introducing modern mechanics that deepen competition and player expression.

Overview
Pong-O is a competitive PvP reinterpretation of Pong set within a circular arena.
Players score points each time their shot crosses the opponent’s half-line, with victory going to the first player to reach 3,000 points. The circular play space transforms traditional positioning and angles, creating constant motion and dynamic rallies.

Core Mechanics

Rally System
To reward evenly matched players and sustained skillful play, Pong-O features a rally multiplier system. As long as a rally continues, both players gain a temporary score multiplier that steadily increases.
Breaking the rally comes at a cost: the player responsible is penalized 50 × the current rally multiplier, reinforcing high-risk, high-reward decision-making.

Shrink System
The Shrink system dynamically reduces the players' paddle size over time but increases their base multiplier, naturally increasing difficulty while incentivizing a higher score potential. For players seeking a more relaxed experience, this system can be disabled prior to the start of a match.
