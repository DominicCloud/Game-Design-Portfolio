Synopsis
Chopstyx is a turn-based RPG that reimagines the classic hand game Chopsticks as a mythological duel against Charon, the Ferryman of the Underworld.
You play as a damned soul crossing the River Styx when Charon, bored with the endless ferrying of the dead, proposes a game. Defeat him in an intentionally unfair match, and you may earn your freedom.

Unbeknownst to Charon, you carry the Rings of the Gods—powerful artifacts capable of disturbing the waters of the Styx and bending the rules of the game in your favor.

Development & Recognition
Chopstyx was developed by a three-person team during the USC Global Game Jam 2026, a 48-hour game development challenge. Within this limited timeframe, we scoped, designed, and built a complete playable experience.
Thanks to the team’s strong collaboration and technical execution, the project received the Best Technical Design Award.

My Role
I served as Producer and Programmer throughout the development sprint. My responsibilities included leading the team, coordinating meetings, managing scope and task allocation, streamlining production workflows, and programming core gameplay systems.
